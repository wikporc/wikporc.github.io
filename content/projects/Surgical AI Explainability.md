My first ever hackathon! We worked on a dataset provided by [Orsi Academy](https://www.orsi-online.com/). The task was to increase the trust in AI-augmented training procedures for young surgeons. Our idea was to introduce an extra layer - eye-tracking to distinguish whether the surgical trainees make mistakes because of wrong technique, or because of distraction - a phenomenon that is well-documented in clinical psychology research journals.

A full-stack RAG-powered platform prototype that helps translate raw military operational needs into standardized procurement requirements — built for the [Belgian Navy Hackathon](https://inno4def.be/navy-hackathon-2/).

- Tackled the problem of "knowledge loss": operational needs vanishing into bureaucracy before ever reaching a technical specification, due to frequent staff rotations.
- Built a RAG platform using LLMs to translate raw operational needs into standardized military requirement formats (DOTMPLFI, KUR).
- Delivered as part of a 6-person team during the 2nd Belgian Navy Hackathon in Zeebrugge.
- Won the "Requirements Clarity" category.

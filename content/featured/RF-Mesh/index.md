A jammer-triangulation system for Electromagnetic Warfare training, streaming real-time positioning data straight to a soldier's tactical display.

- Collaborated with Electromagnetic Warfare specialists to design and prepare a field-ready prototype.
- Built a jammer triangulation pipeline with a live visualization layer, delivered to a soldier's TAK (Team Awareness Kit) via a custom Android plugin.
- Integrated open-source LiDAR elevation data to sharpen triangulation accuracy and flag "safe zones" for uninterrupted friendly communications.
- Pitched the solution to military and civilian stakeholders.
